# Explaining the project in an interview

Use this guide to understand and defend the model before representing the project as your work. Do not describe it as professional transaction experience.

## Questions to practice

- **Why DCF?** It connects a business's ability to generate future cash with the returns its capital providers require.
- **Why not deduct interest from FCFF?** FCFF belongs to debt and equity investors. WACC accounts for financing costs; deducting interest would mix frameworks.
- **Why subtract debt after valuing the business?** Enterprise value belongs to capital providers; the equity bridge allocates that value among claims.
- **Why add only excess cash?** Cash required to operate cannot also be treated as distributable surplus without changing operating assumptions.
- **Why is EBITDA not cash flow?** It omits taxes, capital expenditure, and working-capital needs; adjustments may also exclude recurring costs.
- **Why does terminal cash flow fall?** The model replaces temporary forecast reinvestment ratios with growth/ROIC-based sustainable reinvestment.
- **What is the biggest weakness?** Discount-rate inputs and terminal ROIC are assumed; the model needs market calibration and more detailed accounting adjustments.
- **What would you improve next?** Reconstruct operating working capital, separate depreciation from amortization, estimate diluted shares, and add a dated peer-based WACC/multiples study.
